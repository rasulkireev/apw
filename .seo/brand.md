# Brand and editorial contract

Rasul Kireev's personal digital garden: first-person book notes, programming tutorials, projects, and reflections. Inferred goal: help readers find useful existing writing and optionally subscribe to the personal newsletter; conversion success is not yet verified.

Preserve the author's direct, personal style and broad interests. No keyword stuffing, fabricated experience, generic AI prose, or new AI-authored editorial pages. Existing-content corrections, headings, metadata and internal links are allowed when accurate and useful. Do not rewrite an entire essay merely to satisfy a generic SEO score. Personal topics are not off-topic simply because they are not technical.

Human approval for in-scope shipping is waived; CI and automated reviews are not waived. New editorial writing remains the author's responsibility.
