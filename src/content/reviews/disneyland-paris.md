Main Street, U.S.A. has one advantage over almost any city street. Look up and there’s a pink castle to get your bearings. The shop windows reward a slower walk, and the covered arcades give you a second route back along the same street.

That’s the appeal of Disneyland Paris as a little city for a day. You can pick a landmark, put the map away, and see what catches your eye along the way. The drawback is how many other people are heading for that very same landmark. Leave a little room in the day for a detour.
